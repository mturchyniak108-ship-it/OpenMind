# AI Research Assistant

```text
Question -> Evidence -> Hypothesis -> Experiment -> Result -> Validation -> Interpretation
```

AI can summarize experiments, identify duplicated work, propose controls, generate analysis code, inspect logs, and identify inconsistencies.

AI should not declare a research hypothesis proven.

## Evidence Levels

Level 0: Idea
Level 1: Observed correlation
Level 2: Reproduced observation
Level 3: Controlled experiment
Level 4: Independent holdout validation
Level 5: Behavioral equivalence against baseline

Claims of model replacement require substantially stronger evidence than exploratory correlation.
